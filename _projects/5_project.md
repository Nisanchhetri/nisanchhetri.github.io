Abstract

BERT (Bidirectional Encoder Representations from
Transformers) is one of the most popular Deep Learning
techniques that gained popularity after the transformers.
There are multiple version of BERT models which are designed
for solving various shortcomings. In this paper, we are going
to compare five different BERT models on GLUE (General
Language Understanding Evaluation benchmark) dataset.
This comparison will provide us some insights on practical
implementations of these algorithms in specific problems.
Index Terms—BERT, GLUE, Transformers, Deep Learning
